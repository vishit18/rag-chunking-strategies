# Annual Financial Report 2025

## Company Overview

Acme Technologies is a software company operating across the United Kingdom, Germany, France, and the United States.

The company provides cloud infrastructure and artificial intelligence solutions to enterprise customers.

## Financial Performance

The company generated £5.2 billion in revenue during 2025. This represents an increase of 18% compared with 2024.

Operating profit increased from £800 million in 2024 to £1.1 billion in 2025.

## Revenue by Region

| Region | 2024 Revenue | 2025 Revenue | Growth |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| UK | £1.0B | £1.2B | 20% |
| Germany | £1.4B | £1.8B | 29% |
| France | £0.9B | £1.0B | 11% |
| USA | £1.1B | £1.2B | 9% |

Germany was the fastest-growing major market, with revenue increasing by 29%.

## Future Outlook

Management expects revenue to continue growing during 2026.

Growth is expected to be strongest in European markets, particularly Germany.

The company plans to increase investment in artificial intelligence and cloud infrastructure.
