ACME TECHNOLOGIES — 2025 ANNUAL REPORT

Acme Technologies is a global technology company specializing in artificial intelligence, cloud infrastructure, and enterprise software.

The company reported revenue of £5.2 billion in 2025. This represents an 18% increase compared with the previous year.

The company operates across several major markets including the United Kingdom, Germany, the United States, and France.

Germany generated £1.8 billion in revenue during 2025. The United Kingdom generated £1.2 billion, while the United States generated £1.2 billion.

The company invested £900 million in research and development. A significant portion of this investment was allocated to artificial intelligence and machine learning infrastructure.

Acme Technologies employed 42,000 people at the end of 2025. Employee numbers increased by approximately 11% compared with 2024.

Management expects continued growth in cloud infrastructure and artificial intelligence services during 2026.
